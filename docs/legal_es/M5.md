# KeepMeHonest legal-es / M5-A — Public legal review pilot

**Candidate research increment — NOT legal gold standard or legal-quality validation.** Base: published M4 fork of `Nicolas-Spoel/claims-verifier-core` at `e05dc02293157e42b7a2d882697772b0e755f6ab` (2026-11-04). Nothing in the generic `claims_verifier` package, M2 retrieval, M3 documentary grounding or M4 evaluation was changed.

## Research objective and actual state

M4 passed 12/12 locked developer-written synthetic regression cases, but has **zero expert-adjudicated legal claims**. M5 will eventually produce reproducibly annotated, rights-reviewed public legal statements for evaluating whether legal propositions are supported, partially supported, unsupported, contradicted or context-dependent.

**This delivery is M5-A, not the completion of M5**. It provides:

- `evals/legal_es/m5/pilot_candidates.json`: **36 AI-drafted hypothetical propositions** linked to official public source locators in five domains: constitutional law (9), administrative procedure (10), employment (6), data protection (6), TC constitutional case law (5). This is a **development candidate set**: it is not a random/representative sample, an expert-labeled set, or an independent test/holdout set.
- **Five distinct official document identifiers but only 13 source-section clusters**. Multiple candidates reference the same article/fundamento. A future accuracy study must cluster errors by source/section and use independently sourced holdout cases to avoid an inflated effective sample size.
- Seven *AI-drafted stress dimensions* (not expected legal labels): `SOURCE_SCOPE`, `TEMPORAL`, `EXCEPTION`, `ABSOLUTE_CLAIM`, `PROCEDURAL`, `FACT_GAP`, `CITATION_FIDELITY`. The scenarios contain intentionally questionable legal assertions; **do not assume they are correct, false, or complete** before expert review.
- `m5_queue` an **offline, no-model** corpus validator that rejects fabricated gold labels, evidence hashes, cleared rights, unsupported sources, malicious source URLs, duplicate cases and structurally incomplete data.
- A separate **capture-attestation staging gate** to move a *subset* of cases into M4 `PENDING_EXPERT`, only if local source snapshots actually exist, SHA-256 hashes are computed from those bytes and two distinct named people attest source/version checking and reuse rights. These attestations are human statements: the program **cannot independently authenticate that the official bytes, licenses, qualifications or independence were genuinely checked**. Staging never creates an expert label or an adjudicated result.
- `capture_attestations_TEMPLATE.json` intentionally contains null reviewer/date/license details and `false` checks; **it is designed to FAIL until real work is completed**. Never make it pass by copying the fake credentials used inside unit tests.

## Source rights and legal limits

BOE: <https://www.boe.es/informacion/aviso_legal/> provides reuse conditions including source attribution and non-distortion; licensing applies only to qualifying content and additional document-specific conditions may exist. Its consolidated legislation is **informational and non-official for legal effects**: <https://www.boe.es/datosabiertos/faq/consolidada.php>. Record the authoritative original publication or version as needed and don't confuse the version date with applicability to a concrete event.

TC: do **not** infer that BOE's license applies to the HJ website. Access/reuse conditions for the exact TC response and intended distribution need to be checked separately before capturing or redistributing it. **CENDOJ remains excluded** pending authorization. No private client facts, non-public personal data, production deployment or hosted LLM calls are permitted in M5-A.

## Protocol: how a candidate becomes reviewable

1. **Scope and preregistration**: retain candidate `case_id`, written proposition, invented fact pattern, issue date and precise public source locator. Freeze candidate version before reviewers see the answers. Group by official source/section to avoid leakage. Add other sources only via a new reviewed inventory revision.
2. **Capture the evidence in a controlled local location**: manually verify the official document, section, historical version, text boundaries and document dates. Save the *exact intended evidence section* as UTF-8 plain text in `PUB-M5-001.txt`, etc., outside Git. Recheck original official publication where legal effects matter. `source_version_as_of` in the attestation describes the source version selected, **not legal applicability**.
3. **Review reuse permissions and confidentiality** separately. Record actual reviewers' identifiers, dates, source/rights URLs, a substantive reason, and the limited intended usage. The supplied attestation template is deliberately incomplete; avoid inventing permission or reviewer names. Retain captured bytes under a controlled local policy. Never commit source snapshots, reviewer details or private packets as part of M5-A.
4. **Stage locally**: run `m5_queue` with an explicit verified manifest and capture directory. The tool checks schema/locators, capture file boundaries, computes `argument_sha256` and `evidence_sha256`, and creates M4 **`PENDING_EXPERT`** records plus a separately stored receipt. It never manufactures an expert assessment. Results are written in a newly created private output directory, with no overwrite.
5. **Two independent legal assessments**: reviewers A and B receive the *same frozen proposition, fact pattern, full relevant evidence and relevant surrounding context* independently. Record documentary fidelity, legal relation, temporal/procedural/jurisdictional scope, legal facts availability, reason and locator, without seeing the other's result before submission. `DIRECT_SUPPORT` / `CONTRADICTED` require sufficient case facts under the current M4 gate.
6. **Separate adjudication**: a third reviewer resolves disagreements with explicit reasoning. The existing `m4_review` schema accepts completed, independently recorded A/B reviews and a separate adjudicator. Its structural checks do not guarantee qualifications or substantive quality; obtain appropriate professional review.
7. **Seal and evaluate later**: only an independently adjudicated, permission-cleared, non-confidential inventory could become legal gold. Keep development cases separate from new, non-overlapping held-out sources. Predefine error definitions, abstention rates and coverage **before** running evaluation. Never calculate a legal accuracy estimate from these 36 AI-drafted candidates.

## Commands

Validate candidate inventory. This command **never fetches official text**:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m keepmehonest_legal_es.m5_queue \
  --candidates evals/legal_es/m5/pilot_candidates.json
```

Expected top-level fields: `total_candidates=36`, `unique_source_section_clusters=13`, `source_snapshots_included=0`, `rights_cleared=0`, `expert_gold_cases=0`, `legal_accuracy_measured=false`, `independent_case_sample_claimed=false`.

A **future** staging command, only after human checks and stored real evidence; replace illustrative paths with real local ones:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m keepmehonest_legal_es.m5_queue \
  --candidates evals/legal_es/m5/pilot_candidates.json \
  --stage-manifest /PRIVATE/PATH/verified_capture_attestations.json \
  --capture-root /PRIVATE/PATH/captures \
  --output-dir /PRIVATE/PATH/new_review_batch_01

PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m keepmehonest_legal_es.m4_review \
  --queue /PRIVATE/PATH/new_review_batch_01/m4_pending_reviews.json
```

`--stage-manifest` without complete attestations or exact capture text **must fail**. The output files contain hashes and reviewer identifiers and are not for automatic public distribution.

## M5-A acceptance gates, vs complete M5

For **M5-A accept as candidate infrastructure only**:
- `git apply --check`, `git diff --check`, `tests/legal_es` and Core suite pass on the real fork.
- Corpus validates with **36 unannotated cases / 5 source IDs / 13 clusters**, no duplicate, source-origin or false-gold errors.
- The deliberately incomplete attestation template cannot stage; tests cover forged labels, wrong origins, ID mismatches, unsafe capture paths and omitted human permissions.
- No network or model inference; no change in existing legal-support classification.

**For full M5 gold standard acceptance (NOT met)**:
- Source text/versions captured and checked case-by-case; rights and confidentiality independently cleared.
- Two independent qualified lawyers assess each case; third adjudicates disputes; review independence documented and verified operationally.
- All relevant legal context supplied or undecidable cases labeled as such; substantive annotation audit passes.
- A new source-disjoint held-out dataset and preregistered evaluation protocol are defined; model/legal accuracy is not inferred from M5-A's candidate count.

The candidate suite is an initial *research sample design*, not sufficient to infer real-world reliability or authorize a public beta.
