# KEEP-ME-HONEST-LEGAL-01 / M3-A — Documentary grounding

**Candidate, not production ready.** Against the public fork commit `81ff95e51f41bca2beda1357e124ab41b655d0d0` (M2). Claims Verifier Core is unchanged. License/NOTICE unchanged.

## Purpose and strict interpretation

The goal of M3 is eventual **legal grounding**: determining whether cited rules and judgments justify a legal proposition in its relevant temporal/procedural context. M3-A deliberately addresses **only the necessary first step**: a reproducible claim → exact identifier → exact section → literal quotation trail. It does **not** solve the full M3 objective.

The new `documentary_grounding` field per claim is a separate result from the generic Core's `lexical_verdict`, from M2's identifier-level `linked_cited_sources`, and from legal support.

| `anchor_status` | What it proves | What it does NOT prove |
|---|---|---|
| `DOCUMENTARY_QUOTE_MATCH` | One explicitly cited identifier and section matched one supplied evidence section; one explicit literal quote appears in that section (NFC and whitespace only). | The assertion is entailed, the quote is complete/contextualized, the rule was applicable, or the source is authentic. |
| `SECTION_LOCATED_NO_EXPLICIT_QUOTATION` | Identifier and section matched one supplied evidence item. | That the proposition is supported. |
| `QUOTED_PASSAGE_NOT_FOUND_IN_CITED_SECTION` | The supplied literal quotation was not found in the identified section. | That the legal conclusion is necessarily false. |
| `CITED_SECTION_NOT_SUPPLIED` | The specifically cited section is unavailable among supplied items. | That the section or norm does not exist. |
| `MULTIPLE_EVIDENCE_VERSIONS_AMBIGUOUS` | Several matching sections/versions supplied. | Which version is legally applicable. |
| `NO_EXPLICIT_IDENTIFIER`, `SECTION_NOT_EXPLICITLY_IDENTIFIED`, `SUBARTICLE_SCOPE_UNSUPPORTED`, `MULTIPLE_IDENTIFIERS_REQUIRE_MAPPING`, `MULTIPLE_SECTIONS_AMBIGUOUS`, other guarded statuses | Claim is outside the narrow documentary anchor contract. | Any substantive legal decision. |

Every outcome has `legal_support=NOT_ASSESSED`, `legal_applicability=NOT_ASSESSED`, `argument_quality=NOT_ASSESSED`, `requires_human_review=true`. No overall confidence score. `DOCUMENTARY_QUOTE_MATCH` must never be presented as **legally supported**.

## Conservative boundaries

- Recognizes explicit `BOE-A-...` + `art. 24` / `artículo 24` or TC ECLI + `FJ 1` / `fundamento jurídico 1` / `fallo`. Evidence section identifiers normalize `a24`, `art24`, `FJ1`, `FALLO`. **Does not** infer `BOE-A` from `art. 24 CE`, parse multi-source reasoning, or check numbered subsections (`24.1`) as independently supported.
- Abbreviations `art. 24` and `FJ. 1` are masked **only for sentence splitting** within the legal adapter (same number of characters) and restored before evaluation; the general Core remains untouched.
- Only one source identifier, one cited section, one unambiguous evidence section and one literal quotation can produce the positive **documentary** status. Quotation omissions / ellipses, multiple quotations and under-12-character quotes do not produce positive status.
- BOE consolidated texts are **informational**, not the official legal publication; even a matching quote leaves temporal and legal applicability unassessed. For authoritative legal use consult the original BOE publication: <https://www.boe.es/datosabiertos/faq/consolidada.php>.
- TC selected FJ text is not a substitute for reading the rest of the judgment, its procedural facts, dissent and later case law. A quote need not be the decision's ratio decidendi.
- `TEST_FIXTURE` and `USER_SUPPLIED` evidence remain unauthenticated. A `OFFICIAL_FETCH` origin indicates a scoped read-only fetch, not an independent cryptographic authenticity guarantee. Hashes link report text, not veracity.
- No CENDOJ retrieval, no broad legal search, no NLP/LLM, no confidential cases, no network traffic from `anchor_claim` / `verify_argument`. CLI source retrieval still requires `--allow-official-network` and sends only explicit public identifiers.
- One claim may contain a long quote or complex legal conclusion; the exact quotation can match while the conclusion is wrong. This is an expected outcome, **not** a verifier defect.

## Output and auditability

`verify_argument(...)` adds `claims[i].documentary_grounding` and `coverage.documentary_grounding` to the existing M2 report. `schema_version` becomes `keepmehonest-legal-es/0.3.0`. Existing M2 fields are preserved. The new field carries `claim_sha256`, `evidence_key`, `evidence_sha256`, `source_origin`, cited section, optional quote SHA-256 and raw character span, and explicit review flags. Source text is not added to this new object. Source snapshots must be archived separately, under an approved evidence policy, for full replayability; existing M2 report contains only section and raw-response hashes.

Expect a quote match in article 24, cited section unavailable for article 25, and no explicit section for the third claim. Results must be checked on the actual developer Mac and compared against the official site, not assumed from synthetic fixtures.
5. Run offline with `--text-file examples/legal_es/argument_m3.txt` but no `--boe`: no evidence is available; expect `SOURCES_REQUIRED` and no documentary quote matches.
6. Repeat with a wrong quotation and observe fail-closed status without legal falsity verdict.
7. Review at least two real *public*, non-confidential source examples with independently inspected BOE/TC text, showing both positive documentary and negative cases. Do not commit or publish until the developer Mac runs both suites and output inspection passes.

## Out of scope and M3-B prerequisites

To conclude a legal proposition is backed by an authority, M3-B needs a gold-standard Spanish-law corpus with lawyer-labeled propositions and relevant paragraphs, negative/conflicting cases, time and jurisdiction labels, and documented rights/privacy/deployment path. Only then evaluate constrained semantic/legal support (with expert oversight). No model benchmarking merely to choose a model; no deployment promises.
