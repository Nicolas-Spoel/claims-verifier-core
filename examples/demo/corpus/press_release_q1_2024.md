# Nimbus Logística presents first-quarter 2024 results

Lima, May 10, 2024. Nimbus Logística reported that its revenue for the first quarter of 2024 grew 9% compared with the same period of the previous year.

The fleet expanded to 340 trucks after the addition of 30 new units.

The company is evaluating a new distribution center in Trujillo, which could open in 2025 subject to obtaining municipal permits.
